The first Nordic-RSE conference has been canceled

Initially we have planned for October 21-22, 2020 and our attempt to move the conference to May 2021 did not seem realistic enough due to uncertainties around the COVID-19 situation. The conference is cancelled.

To keep up the momentum, we held an online unconference June 29-30, 2021. You can find recordings of the invited talks on our Youtube channel Playlist
